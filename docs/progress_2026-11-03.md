## rPPG-Toolbox on Modal (`modal_app/toolbox.py`)
Works for unsupervised, pretrained `only_test`, and `train_and_test`. Python 3.10 image, torch 2.1.2 cu121,
`mamba_ssm` stubbed. One command per experiment; outputs in `/data/toolbox_runs/<config>_<tag>/`.

### Unsupervised methods on SCAMPS example (10 clips, toolbox's own implementations)
| GREEN | ICA | PBV | LGI | OMIT | POS | CHROM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MAE 0.0 | 5.6 | 6.0 | 14.4 | 14.4 | 16.0 | 18.8 |
Confirms the earlier finding: SCAMPS pulse is near-achromatic; chrominance methods (POS/CHROM) fail on it
by construction, GREEN is exact. Not a property of real skin.

### Pretrained TS-CAN (SCAMPS weights) on SCAMPS example: MAE 0.0 (in-domain, sanity only).

### Training: TS-CAN on the 300-clip SCAMPS slice
Split 80/10/10 by file order, 5 epochs, batch 4, A10G. **23 min end to end** (≈12 min preprocessing,
≈8 min training). Held-out SCAMPS test (30 clips): **MAE 1.93, RMSE 9.3, r 0.96**.
Weights: `/data/toolbox_runs/SCAMPS_SCAMPS_UBFC-rPPG_TSCAN_BASIC_scamps300/PreTrainedModels/SCAMPS_SCAMPS_UBFC_tscan_Epoch4.pth`
(local copy `models/trained/tscan_scamps300_ep5.pth`, git-ignored). Synthetic-only; real-data test pending UBFC.

## Pretrained models on rppg_biases (`modal_app/pretrained_rppg_biases.py`, native 15 fps)
8 toolbox checkpoints (TS-CAN / EfficientPhys / PhysNet × SCAMPS / UBFC / PURE), ROI crops → 72×72,
toolbox preprocessing, cumsum, our harmonic-scored fused-spectrum HR.

| model | MAE | bias | r |
|---|---|---|---|
| SCAMPS_TSCAN (fft) | 13.9 | +9.0 | 0.08 |
| SCAMPS_PhysNet | 16.1 | −16.0 | 0.12 |
| PURE_PhysNet (fft) | 17.6 | −2.1 | −0.08 |
| UBFC/PURE TS-CAN, EfficientPhys, PhysNet | 18–23 | −9 … −22 | ≈0 |
| our classical best (green, Welch, harmonic 0.5) | 11.6 | −2.0 | 0.13 |
| constant mean | 4.5 | 0 | 0 |

Reading: nothing, learned or classical, recovers per-subject HR from these crops (authors' own r ≈ 0).
The large negative bias of the 30 fps-trained models at 15 fps input suggests temporal-scale mismatch;
a 2× temporal-upsampling run is in progress (`--upsample`). Either way rppg_biases stays a **bias/fairness**
check, not an accuracy benchmark.
